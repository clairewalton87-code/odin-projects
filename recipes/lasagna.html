A classic, hearty homemade lasagna with rich meat sauce and layers of cheese.

Ingredients

  • 1 pound of ground beef
  • 1 box lasagna noodles
  • 2 cups mozzeralle cheese
  • 24oz of marinara sauce

Steps

  1. Boil the pasta noodles according to the package instructions.
  2. Brown the ground beef in a pan and stir in marinare sauce
  3. Layer sauce, noodles, and cheese in a baking dish.
  4. Bake at 375°F (190°C) for 25 minutes.
